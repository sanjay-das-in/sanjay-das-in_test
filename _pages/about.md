I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Oak Ridge National Laboratory working at the intersection of **AI for Science & Operations**, **hardware-aware machine learning**, and **AI system reliability**. My research focuses on developing **robust, trustworthy, and safety-aware AI systems** that can be deployed in mission-critical scientific and operational environments.

My work bridges the gap between **low-level system behavior**—including hardware faults, quantization effects, and architectural constraints—and **high-level AI performance**, particularly in large-scale models such as LLMs. I study how subtle perturbations at the hardware and system level can impact model behavior, security, and reliability, and develop methods to **detect, analyze, and mitigate these vulnerabilities**.

More broadly, I design **optimization-driven and learning-based frameworks** for:
- AI-assisted scientific hypothesis generation  
- Robustness evaluation and adversarial analysis  
- Safe integration of AI into operational workflows  

My goal is to enable the next generation of AI systems that are not only powerful, but **dependable, interpretable, and aligned with real-world safety requirements** across domains such as scientific discovery, healthcare, and critical infrastructure.
